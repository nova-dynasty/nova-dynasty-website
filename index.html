Server Rules

Keep the crew safe, fair and fun.

Tricking, baiting or misleading members in trades is an instant ban. Use official middlemen and report suspicious users to staff.

No harassment, hate speech or toxicity. Win with skill, lose with grace, and help newer players learn.

Trading Roblox items for real money, other games' items or accounts is not allowed, as it breaks Roblox's Terms of Use.

No repeated messages, mass pings, unsolicited advertising or invite links. Keep each topic in its proper channel.
